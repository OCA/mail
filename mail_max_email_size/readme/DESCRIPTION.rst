When an outgoing email exceeds a maximum size, the attachments of its document
are sent as download links at the bottom of the email instead of as
attachments.

Attachments of the message itself stay attached, as their links would break
once the message is deleted.

This module is a backport from Odoo SA and as such, it is not included in the
OCA CLA. That means we do not have a copy of the copyright on it like all other
OCA modules.
