This module lets the participants of a Discuss call draw on a shared
screen, in the spirit of the annotations of Google Meet, so they can
point at what they talk about: a field of a form, a line of a report, a
button to click.

This first version draws vanishing ink: a stroke stays visible for two
seconds, then fades out. Each participant draws in their own color, and
the strokes of the other participants are labelled with their name.

The strokes are sent through the existing call connection (peer-to-peer
or SFU server). Nothing is stored on the server and no screen content is
changed: the ink is drawn over the video of the shared screen.

.. IMPORTANT::
   This is an alpha version, the data model and design can change at any time without warning.
   Only for development or testing purpose, do not use in production.
   `More details on development status <https://odoo-community.org/page/development-status>`_

Usage
=====

1. Join a Discuss call in which a participant shares their screen.
2. In the call actions, click **Draw on Shared Screen** (pencil icon).
   It is in the **More** menu when the action bar is full.
3. Press and drag on the shared screen: your ink appears for every
   participant who watches it, then fades out.
4. Click **Stop Drawing on Shared Screen** to use the pointer normally
   again, for example to focus or pin a participant.

The presenter sees the ink on the preview of their own screen in the
call view, once they choose to show it (Odoo hides it by default to
avoid an infinite mirror effect).

Known issues / Roadmap
======================

- The ink is drawn in the Odoo call view, over the video of the shared
  screen. A web page cannot draw over the windows of other applications,
  so the presenter does not see it on the screen they share, only on its
  preview in the call view.
- The ink is not part of call recordings.
- Persistent pen, eraser, undo and clear tools, with a permission to
  choose who can draw, would make it a complete annotation feature.
