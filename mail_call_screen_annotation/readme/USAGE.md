1. Join a Discuss call in which a participant shares their screen.
2. In the call actions, click **Draw on Shared Screen** (pencil icon). It is
   in the **More** menu when the action bar is full.
3. Press and drag on the shared screen: your ink appears for every
   participant who watches it, then fades out.
4. Click **Stop Drawing on Shared Screen** to use the pointer normally again,
   for example to focus or pin a participant.

The presenter sees the ink on the preview of their own screen in the call
view, once they choose to show it (Odoo hides it by default to avoid an
infinite mirror effect).
